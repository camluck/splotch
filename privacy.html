Privacy Policy

Splotch · Last updated October 18, 2026

The short version: Splotch does not collect, store, share or sell any information about you or your child. Everything you make stays on your device.

What Splotch collects

Nothing. Splotch has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. It does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • Wallpapers your child makes, kept in the app's own "My Walls" gallery.
  • The child's first name (optional), used only so Splotch can say hello.
  • Stars, streaks, badges, prizes and settings.

All of this stays on the device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Permissions Splotch may ask for

  • Photos (add only): to save finished wallpapers to your photo library. Splotch cannot read your existing photos this way.
  • Photo picker: in Scrapbook, your child can choose photos with Apple's picker. Only the photos they pick are used, and only on the device.
  • Camera: only when your child taps Camera in Scrapbook.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: only while the "Hold & talk" button is held, to turn spoken ideas into text. Recognition runs on the device when the iPhone supports it.
  • Notifications: off by default. A grown-up can turn on one daily challenge reminder from the Grown-ups screen.

Imagine (AI pictures)

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Splotch's Imagine mode uses Apple's on-device image generator, with Apple's built-in safety filters. Ideas and pictures are processed on the device and are not sent to Splotch. Other iPhones use an offline emoji version.

Grown-up controls

Sharing, deleting wallpapers and the settings screen are behind a grown-up check. Splotch has no links to websites and no social features.

Children's privacy

Splotch is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by not collecting any personal information.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
