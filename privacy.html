Privacy Policy 🔒

Last updated October 18, 2026

🛡️
The short version: Splotchy doesn't collect, store, share or sell any information about you or your child. Everything you make stays on your device.

🙅 What Splotchy collects

Nothing. Splotchy has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. It does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

📱 What stays on your device

  • Wallpapers your child makes, kept in the app's own "My Stuff" gallery (called "My Walls" in version 1.0).
  • The child's first name (optional), used only so Splotchy can say hello.
  • Stars, streaks, badges, prizes and settings.

All of this stays on the device and is deleted when you delete the app.

🔑 Permissions Splotchy may ask for

  • Photos (add only): to save finished wallpapers to your photo library. Splotchy cannot read your existing photos this way.
  • Photo picker: in Scrapbook, your child can choose photos with Apple's picker. Only the photos they pick are used, and only on the device.
  • Camera: only when your child taps Camera in Scrapbook.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: only while the "Hold & talk" button is held, to turn spoken ideas into text. Recognition runs on the device when the iPhone supports it.
  • Notifications: off by default. A grown-up can turn on one daily challenge reminder from the Grown-ups screen.

💭 Imagine (AI pictures)

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Splotchy's Imagine mode uses Apple's on-device image generator, with Apple's built-in safety filters. Ideas and pictures are processed on the device and are not sent to Splotchy. Other iPhones use an offline emoji version.

🔒 Grown-up controls

Sharing, deleting wallpapers and the settings screen are behind a grown-up check. Splotchy has no links to websites and no social features.

🧒 Children's privacy

Splotchy is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by not collecting any personal information.

📝 Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
