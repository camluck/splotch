Is the AI safe for kids?

Imagine uses Apple's on-device image generator with Apple's own safety filters. Older iPhones use an offline emoji version.

How do we set the wallpaper?

Tap Done, then Save. Then open Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper → Photos.

Will it nag my kid?

No. The daily reminder stays off unless you turn it on.
