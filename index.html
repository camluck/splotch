Splotch

Make your own phone wallpapers!

Splotch is a kid-friendly iPhone app for making phone wallpapers: draw, color by number, imagine new things, write your name in fancy letters, and more, with Splotch the paint blob cheering you on.

Need help? Email cameron.t.luck@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

How do I set a wallpaper?

  1. Make something in Splotch, tap DONE, then SAVE IT.
  2. Open the iPhone's Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper.
  3. Tap Photos and pick the newest picture.

Why doesn't Imagine make real pictures on my phone?

Realistic Imagine pictures need an iPhone with Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer) with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Other iPhones use Splotch's offline emoji version.

Talking to Splotch doesn't work

A grown-up can allow the microphone and speech recognition for Splotch in Settings → Privacy & Security. You can always type ideas instead.

Where are the grown-up settings?

Scroll to the bottom of Splotch's home screen and tap Grown-ups. From there you can change the name, turn sounds and the daily reminder on or off, or reset progress.
