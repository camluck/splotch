For grown-ups

Built so you don't have to hover.

What does it collect?

Nothing. No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Wallpapers stay on the device. Privacy policy.

Is the AI safe for kids?

Imagine uses Apple's on-device image generator with Apple's built-in safety filters. Phones without Apple Intelligence use an offline emoji version.

What's behind the grown-up check?

Sharing, deleting and settings ask a quick multiplication question first. There are no links out of the app.

Will it nag my kid?

No. The daily reminder is off unless you turn it on, and it skips the day once the challenge is done.

How do they set the wallpaper?

Tap Done, then Save. Then Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper → Photos.

How much is it?

Free. No in-app purchases, no subscription.
