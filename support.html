📲 How do I set my wallpaper?
  1. Make something, tap DONE, then SAVE IT.
  2. Open Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper.
  3. Tap Photos and pick your newest picture.
💭 Why does Imagine use emoji on my phone?

Real Imagine pictures need an iPhone 15 Pro or newer with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Other iPhones get Splotchy's emoji version.

🎤 Splotchy can't hear me!

A grown-up can turn on the microphone and speech for Splotchy in Settings → Privacy & Security. You can always type your ideas too.

🔒 Where are the grown-up settings?

Scroll to the bottom of Splotchy's home screen and tap Grown-ups. You can change the name, sounds, the daily reminder, or reset progress.

🛡️ Does Splotchy collect any data?

Nope, nothing at all. Read the privacy policy.
